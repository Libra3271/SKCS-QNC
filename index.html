// 03 Process

How QNC Works

  1. 01

    Apply

    Sign up for the QNC program.

  2. 02

    Team Matching

    Find teammates and form a team for your project.

  3. 03

    Idea Development

    Flesh out your project idea with your team and plan its development.

    The executive team will check in on your progress regularly, and each team must complete the provided template. Get the templates →

  4. 04

    Mid-Break Advising Session

    At the midpoint, get feedback and mentoring from professors and Project Managers.

  5. 05

    Project Development

    Build and finish your project based on that feedback.

  6. 06

    Final Presentation & QNC Award Ceremony

    Present your project at the end of the semester. Professors evaluate the projects and select the best ones.

Weekly Check-in Sheet

The progress report each team submits weekly to their PM / Advisor. Add each new week at the top, and keep earlier entries instead of deleting them.

  • Team Profile
  • Milestones & Roadmap
  • Open Action Items
  • Weekly Check-in Log
Download

How to fill them out

  • Use one shared document per team.
  • Keep answers brief: 1–3 sentences per question.
  • Back up claims with an observation, source, test result, or artifact, and label untested claims as “Assumption.”
  • If you don't know an answer, write “To verify” with an owner and a deadline.
  • Every action item needs one owner, a due date, and a clear completion condition.
  • If you're blocked, tell us right away instead of waiting for the weekly check-in.
